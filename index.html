About Me

Avery_Bio.txt — Notepad

I've always been known as the "IT Guy" in my friend group… and I would have to agree with them.

Tech in general has always been a huge part of my life, even as a child playing Minecraft on my Ipad.I am also very into Art, Ive been keeping sketchbooks of my art since I could pick up a pencil. I ended up going to EVIT for Digital Animation, this let me discover the world of 3D computer graphics, I absolutely adored it. I ended up talking to my teacher about other places I could go that could help me further my skills, this led me to taking a Software and App design class. This class really pushed me to understand the backend of computer software and fueled my interest in tech so much more. I began to understand that I could combine my Art experience and my Coding experience to make real projects. This led me to design my own Horror game. The experience I've gotten from these two classes have not only let me experience and learn about topics I am really passionate about, but also let me figure out what I want to do in the future.

I am very interested in being a Technical Artist. I am actively seeking to take a course by Vertex Schools on Technical Art, in addition to being a Future Computer Science major at CGCC. I am passionate about what I want to do and as a hard worker I know I have a place in the industry of Tech and/Or Technical Art. So have a look around at all my hard work and projects that I have done and please contact me if you have any questions!
